How it works

DrawDesign on the build grid

An 8 × 8 canvas, one finger, one color. Kids tap out a picture: a rocket, a ghost, their initials, anything. 8 minutes of focus, not 8 seconds of scrolling.

PrintWe print the exact pixels

Every filled cell becomes a raised voxel on a 32 × 32 mm tile, printed in solid single-filament plastic. No smoothing, no editing. What they drew is what they get.

ShipIt arrives with their name on it

The tile ships with a spec sheet carrying their design's serial number. A real object they engineered, not a toy they were sold.
