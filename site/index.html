How it works

DrawDesign on the build grid

A 16 × 16 canvas, one finger, one color. Every cell is a real 2 mm voxel, so the drawing detail matches what the machine can actually print. 8 minutes of focus, not 8 seconds of scrolling.

PrintWe slice it on a Bambu Lab A1

The blueprint exports as a real 3D model file (3MF, the Bambu Studio format). We slice it and every filled voxel prints raised on a 32 × 32 mm tile. No smoothing, no editing. What they drew is what they get.

ShipIt arrives with their name on it

The tile ships with a spec sheet carrying their design's serial number. A real object they engineered, not a toy they were sold.
