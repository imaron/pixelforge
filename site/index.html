01

How it works

1They draw

On a simple grid, one finger, one color. Every square they fill becomes a raised block on their tile. Minutes of happy focus, not another screen to scroll.

2We print it

Our 3D printer prints exactly what they drew, in the color they picked. No edits, no smoothing. What they made is what they get.

3It arrives

The printed tile lands in your mailbox with a card carrying their design's secret code. A real thing they made with their own imagination.
